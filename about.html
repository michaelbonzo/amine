Who We Are

A Zimbabwean gold miner with a purpose.

Asiphileni Gold Mine is a Zimbabwean gold producer built on skilled local teams, disciplined operations and a long-term commitment to the region we call home.

Our name, Asiphileni, means “let us live”. It is a promise that the gold we bring to the surface will build livelihoods, support families and leave the land and community stronger than we found them.

  • Locally rooted, professionally run
  • Safety-first culture on every shift
  • Transparent, traceable gold production

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To mine gold safely, efficiently and responsibly, creating lasting value for our people, our partners and our community.

Our Vision

To be recognised as one of Zimbabwe’s most respected gold producers: admired for safety, integrity and community impact.

Our Values

Safety above all. Integrity in every ounce. Respect for people and land. Excellence through teamwork.
