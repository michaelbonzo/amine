Our Story

Gold Mining
With a Difference.
Asiphileni.

Asiphileni means “let us live”. The name is a promise: that the gold we bring to the surface builds livelihoods, supports families and leaves the land and community stronger than we found them. We combine modern mining methods with deep respect for the people and environment around us.
